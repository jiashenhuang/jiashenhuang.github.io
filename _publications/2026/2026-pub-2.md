abstract: >-
    Why do people trust some AI systems more than others? This study examines how institutional trust shapes perceptions of domestic and global AI. It shows that trust depends not only on performance, but also on governance contexts. Higher institutional trust strengthens emotional connections to domestic AI and shifts evaluations in its favor, highlighting how social environments shape trust in emerging technologies.
